About Me

Vol. 06 · PortfolioTHE LUA CIASCA TIMESVideo · Motion · Story

Hi, I’m Lua.

I edit videos and make them move. I’m curious about almost everything, and I’ve never been very good at doing just one thing.

It started with a camera. The first time I held one, I realised I could keep a feeling and hand it to someone who wasn’t even there. So I learned photography, then filmmaking, then editing, then motion design. Not to fill a resume, but because each one let me tell a story a little better.

Outside of work I’m usually watching films, taking photos or playing guitar. It all ends up in my edits somehow.

My mom always said I was a good liar, because whenever I tell a story people believe it. I prefer to call it storytelling. For the past 6 years I’ve been doing it for brands, agencies and people with something to say.

Say hello.

If something here made you feel something, or you just want to talk about films, write to me.
